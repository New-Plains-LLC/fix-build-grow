## Crawlspace & Basement Waterproofing in Edmond, OK

Moisture control is essential to maintaining a healthy, stable foundation. In Edmond's climate, crawlspaces and basements are particularly vulnerable to humidity, groundwater intrusion, and condensation.

### Crawlspace Encapsulation

A vapor barrier on the crawlspace floor isn't enough. Full encapsulation includes:

- **Heavy-duty vapor barrier** (20-mil minimum) covering the floor and extending up foundation walls
- **Sealing all crawlspace vents** to control humidity
- **Insulating crawlspace walls** (not the floor above) for energy efficiency
- **Installing a dehumidifier** sized for the crawlspace volume
- **Sealing all penetrations** where plumbing, electrical, and HVAC lines enter

### Basement Waterproofing

For homes with basements, we address water intrusion from multiple angles:

**Interior Solutions:**
- Interior drain tile systems around the perimeter
- Sump pump installation with battery backup
- Wall vapor barriers and drainage panels
- Crack injection for active leaks

**Exterior Solutions:**
- Excavation and waterproof membrane application
- Exterior drain tile at the footing level
- Window well drainage improvements
- Backfill with non-expansive, free-draining soil

### Benefits of a Dry Crawlspace or Basement

- **Better indoor air quality** – Crawlspace moisture directly affects the air you breathe. A dry crawlspace means fewer allergens and no musty odors.
- **Lower energy bills** – Dry insulation performs better. You'll see savings on both heating and cooling.
- **No mold or mildew** – Without moisture, mold can't grow. This protects both your home and your family's health.
- **Pest prevention** – Termites, carpenter ants, and rodents need moisture. A dry crawlspace is an uninviting environment for them.
- **Higher property value** – A properly waterproofed basement or crawlspace adds usable storage space and peace of mind for buyers.

### When to Waterproof

The best time to waterproof is before you have a problem. But if you're already noticing musty odors, visible mold, or standing water, don't wait — every day of moisture exposure causes cumulative structural damage.
