## Foundation Leveling & Stabilization in Moore, OK

When your foundation sinks or settles unevenly, the entire structure of your home is affected. Moore's clay-rich soil expands when wet and contracts during drought, creating constant movement that can destabilize your foundation over time.

### Signs Your Foundation Needs Leveling

- Floors that slope noticeably from one side of a room to the other
- Gaps between walls and ceilings or walls and floors
- Doors and windows that stick, bind, or won't close properly
- Cabinets pulling away from walls in the kitchen
- Visible gaps between the foundation and exterior walls
- Separating drywall seams and cracked ceiling corners

### Our Leveling Methods

**Steel Push Piers** – Driven deep through unstable soil to bedrock or load-bearing strata. Ideal for heavier foundations and deep-seated settling.

**Helical Piers** – Screwed into the ground to provide tension and compression support. Best for lighter structures and areas with high water tables.

**Slab Jacking (Mudjacking)** – A cementitious grout is pumped beneath the slab to lift it back to its original position. Cost-effective for modest settling.

**Polyurethane Foam Injection** – High-density foam expands beneath the slab, lifting it precisely. Lighter, faster-curing, and more controlled than mudjacking.

### The Leveling Process

1. **Assessment** – We survey your foundation and measure elevation differences across the structure
2. **Diagnosis** – Determine the cause: soil conditions, drainage issues, tree roots, or construction deficiencies
3. **Plan** – Recommend the appropriate method based on soil type, foundation construction, and budget
4. **Lift** – Install piers or inject material to raise the foundation in controlled increments
5. **Verify** – Re-measure elevations to confirm the foundation is level and stable
6. **Backfill** – Restore the work area to its original condition

### Why Soil in Moore Matters

Central Oklahoma is known for its **Hefner silt loam and Dougherty loam soil** — both highly expansive. These soils can swell up to 15% with moisture and shrink just as much during dry spells. This constant movement is the #1 cause of foundation issues in the region.
