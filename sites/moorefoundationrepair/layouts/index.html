Signs You Need Foundation Repair

Cracks in Walls

Hairline to wide cracks in drywall, brick, or block walls — especially around doors and windows.

Sticking Doors & Windows

Doors and windows that stick or won't close properly often indicate foundation movement.

Uneven Floors

Sloping, sagging, or bouncy floors can signal serious foundation issues underneath.

Gaps Around Exterior

Visible gaps between your foundation wall and the soil, or between brick mortar joints.

Chimney Tilting

A chimney pulling away from the house or leaning is a red flag for foundation problems.

Basement Water

Water intrusion through foundation cracks or walls can worsen structural damage over time.
